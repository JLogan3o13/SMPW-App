1. Set Up Your Device

Do this once before your first shift. It takes about 5 minutes.

1.1 Install DuckDuckGo
Why DuckDuckGo? You get a new car number every day, and the driver page remembers the last one you used. DuckDuckGo’s Fire button clears that in one tap, so you start each day fresh. Everyone using the same browser also means the Key Team can help you more easily.
  1. Open the App Store (iPhone) or Google Play Store (Android).
  2. Search for DuckDuckGo. Look for the orange duck icon, made by DuckDuckGo, and tap Install (iPhone: Get).
  3. Open DuckDuckGo. If it asks to be your default browser, you can tap Skip – it isn’t required.
  4. When a driver page asks to use your location, tap Allow. The map can’t follow you without it.
1.2 Add the driver pages as Favorites

Favorites show up as tiles every time you open DuckDuckGo, so you never have to type a link.

  1. In DuckDuckGo, open the first link below.
  2. Tap the three lines (☰) at the top or bottom of the screen, then tap Add Bookmark.
  3. A box pops up at the bottom of the screen. Turn on the Add to Favorites slider.
  4. Tap Edit Bookmark and give it a clear name, like “F1 Driver Form” or “Raiders Training”, then save.
  5. Repeat for each link you’ll need. From now on, the shortcuts appear on the start screen every time you open DuckDuckGo.

1.3 Getting around the map
  1. Landscape mode: turn your device sideways, then tap the chevron (›) on the right side of the map to hide the buttons and see more of the map. Tap it again to bring the buttons back.
  2. Status bar: green dots next to Map loaded, GPS: active and Route: On route mean everything is working.
  3. Directions: the green box at the top left shows your next turn and how far away it is.
  4. Route line: a solid green line means you’re on route. A red dashed line means you’ve gone off route – it turns green again when you’re back on it.
  5. Re-center jumps the map back to where you are.
  6. View: 3D / 2D switches between a tilted and a flat map.
  7. Follow: On keeps the map moving and turning with you while you drive.
  8. Back to Form takes you back to your Driver page.
1.4 Preview your route (Training page)

The Training page plays a video drive-through of each route, so you know what to expect before you drive it. Use it if you’re new, or if you’ve been given a Zone you haven’t driven before.

  1. Open your Training Favorite (see 1.2). The purple bar at the top tells you you’re in training mode, not the live Driver page.
  2. Pick your Zone and Location. There’s no car number or passenger count here.
  3. Tap Preview route to destination to watch the drive out, or Preview return to Hub to watch the drive back.
  4. Tap View parking photo to see exactly where to stop.
Nothing on the Training page is recorded. You can watch the videos at home, as often as you like.

2. Your Day

What happens from check-in to check-out.

2.1 When you arrive
  1. First thing every morning: open DuckDuckGo and tap the Fire button (🔥), then confirm. This clears yesterday’s car number. Your Favorites stay saved.

    The Fire button is the flame next to the address bar.

  2. The Key Team will give you a car number for the day.
  3. The Key Team will assign you a Zone to drive to and pick up from.
If DuckDuckGo ever offers to “Fireproof” lv-transportation.org, tap No. A fireproofed site isn’t cleared, so yesterday’s car number would stick around.
New Zone you haven’t driven before? Watch its drive-through on the Training page first (see 1.4).
2.2 Filling out the Driver page

Everything on the Driver page is a drop-down – tap a box and pick from the list.

  1. Select Zone – the Zone the Key Team gave you.
  2. Select Location – where you’re going. A parking photo pops up showing exactly where to stop. The photo may include extra parking directions, so review it carefully. Then tap the × to close it.
  3. Select Car Number – pick the number the Key Team gave you. The list comes from today’s Driver Tracking board.
    • Your device remembers it, so after the first time it just shows in green (like Car 99).
    • Wrong number showing? Tap Not you? Change car.
    • Your number isn’t in the list? Ask the Key Team.
  4. # of Passengers – how many people are in your car for this trip. Use 0 if you’re driving empty.
  5. Tap Navigate to Destination to drive out, or Return to Hub to drive back.
The two buttons stay gray until every box is filled in. If you can’t tap them, check for an empty drop-down.
2.3 Keep the map open

On every trip, out and back, keep the map open until you’ve parked. The app uses your location to keep your status up to date. When you pull into the Hub lot, it sets you back to Available automatically – if you close the map early, that won’t happen.

When you arrive somewhere and need the Driver page again, tap Back to Form on the map.

MORNING Drop-offs
  1. While in line, fill out your Driver page (see 2.2).
  2. Pull to the front and show your Driver page to the Key Team member.
  3. Once they confirm, tap Navigate to Destination and start driving.
  4. At the drop-off location, the Key Driver will confirm your drop-off.
  5. Tap Back to Form on the map.
  6. Change # of Passengers for the trip back:
    • Coming back empty → 0
    • Bringing people back → how many
  7. Tap Return to Hub and keep the map open all the way into the Hub lot.
No WhatsApp message needed.
MID-DAY Volunteer shuttles
  1. Same as the morning: fill out your Driver page, show the Key Team, then tap Navigate to Destination.
  2. At the drop-off location, the Key Driver will confirm your drop-off and send volunteers to your car.
  3. Tap Back to Form and set # of Passengers to the number of volunteers you’re bringing back.
  4. Tap Return to Hub and keep the map open all the way into the Hub lot.
EVENING Pickups & check-out
  1. Set # of Passengers to 0 and tap Navigate to Destination.
  2. At the pickup location, the Key Driver will send volunteers to your car.
  3. Tap Back to Form and set # of Passengers to the number of people in your car.
  4. Tap Return to Hub and keep the map open all the way into the Hub lot.
  5. Back at the Hub, a Key Team member will:
    • confirm your drop-off
    • check you out for the evening
    • collect your car number and lanyard
ISSUES When to use WhatsApp

You only need WhatsApp if something goes wrong:

  • An accident
  • An unexpected road closure
  • Anything else the Key Team needs to handle
Pull over safely before sending a message. Never text while driving.
